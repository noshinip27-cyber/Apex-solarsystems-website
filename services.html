Our Services

Comprehensive solar solutions engineered for performance, reliability, and long-term savings.

Rooftop Solar

Our rooftop solar systems transform idle roof space into powerful, revenue-generating energy plants. Whether it's your home, shop, school, or factory, we design and install systems that slash your electricity bills from day one.

  • Systems from 1 kW to 500 kW and above
  • Grid-tied and off-grid options available
  • Eligible for government subsidies (MNRE)
  • Net metering & TANGEDCO approvals handled
  • 25-year panel performance warranty

Ground Mounted Solar

For large plots, agricultural land, and industrial campuses, our ground-mounted solar systems deliver maximum energy output at competitive cost per watt. We handle everything from feasibility studies to energisation.

  • MW-scale project capability
  • Optimal tilt angle and row spacing design
  • Hot-dip galvanised mounting structures
  • Remote monitoring & SCADA integration
  • Agricultural solar (agri-voltaic) solutions
