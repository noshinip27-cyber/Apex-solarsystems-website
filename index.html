Who We Are

Apex Solar Systems is a pioneering force in renewable energy, delivering cutting-edge photovoltaic (PV) module solutions and comprehensive EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) services across Tamil Nadu. We also trade as Apex Rocks & Minerals, bringing the same commitment to quality and reliability across industries.

Founded on the belief that clean energy should be accessible to all, we serve homeowners, institutions, industries, and agricultural communities. Our team of certified engineers and technicians ensures every installation is safe, efficient, and built to last for 25+ years.

From a single rooftop panel to multi-megawatt ground-mounted arrays, Apex Solar Systems is your trusted partner for every stage of the solar journey — from initial consultation and design to final commissioning and ongoing maintenance.

Our Services

Comprehensive solar solutions engineered for performance, reliability, and long-term savings.

Rooftop Solar

Our rooftop solar systems transform idle roof space into powerful, revenue-generating energy plants. Whether it's your home, shop, school, or factory, we design and install systems that slash your electricity bills from day one.

  • Systems from 1 kW to 500 kW and above
  • Grid-tied and off-grid options available
  • Eligible for government subsidies (MNRE)
  • Net metering & TANGEDCO approvals handled
  • 25-year panel performance warranty

Ground Mounted Solar

For large plots, agricultural land, and industrial campuses, our ground-mounted solar systems deliver maximum energy output at competitive cost per watt. We handle everything from feasibility studies to energisation.

  • MW-scale project capability
  • Optimal tilt angle and row spacing design
  • Hot-dip galvanised mounting structures
  • Remote monitoring & SCADA integration
  • Agricultural solar (agri-voltaic) solutions

EPC Solutions

Our full-scope Engineering, Procurement & Construction service means you deal with one trusted partner from concept to commissioning. We manage procurement, civil work, electrical installation, testing, and approvals — so you don't have to.

  • Detailed engineering design & simulations
  • Tier 1 panel and inverter procurement
  • Civil, structural & electrical work
  • Commissioning, testing & handover
  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
